# Predicting-the-Impact-of-Social-Media-and-Gaming-on-daily-mood
Research Question: How accurately can hours of social media and gaming usage, combined with self-reported psychological responses, predict the day-to-day mental health outcomes (daily mood) of individuals?


## Objective

- To analyze the data of different people who had different responses to how much time they spend on social media and playing video games. 
- To make conclusions and predictions based on the data about who is going to be affected and how they will be affected based on their usage of social media and time playing video games.

## Dataset 
- We created our own dataset by sending out a survey to over a 100 people and getting responses back from over a 70 people.
- The dataset can be accessed here: [[Final_project.csv](url)](https://github.com/Ojas2011/Impact-of-Social-Media-Gaming-on-daily-mood/blob/Master/Final_project.csv)

## Variables

| Header                                                      | Description                                                                                                   |
|-------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Timestamp                                                   | Date and time when the survey was submitted                                                                   |
| Age Range                                                   | Participant's age group (e.g., Under 18, 18-21, 22-25, 26+)                                                   |
| Gender                                                      | Participant's gender                                                                                          |
| Are you a student?                                          | Whether the participant is a student (Yes/No)                                                                 |
| How many hours per day do you spend on social media?        | Estimated daily social media use (e.g., Less than 1 hour, 1-3 hours, etc.)                                    |
| Which platforms do you use the most?                        | Main social media platforms used                                                                              |
| How often do you check social media?                        | Frequency of checking social media (e.g., Every hour, A few times a day, Constantly)                          |
| What do you mostly do on social media?                      | Primary activities on social media (Scroll/watch content, Chat with friends, Follow news, Post content, etc.) |
| Do you play video games?                                    | Whether the participant plays video games (Yes/No/Maybe)                                                      |
| If yes, how many hours per day do you game?             | Estimated daily gaming time                                                                                   |
| What type of games do you play most?                             | Preferred genre/type of video games (e.g., Casual/mobile, Competitive FPS, Multiplayer online)                |
| Do you play games late at night (past midnight)?                                | Frequency of gaming past midnight (Never, Sometimes, Often, Always)                                                         |
| [Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat, Sun] (Mood ratings)          | Self-rated daily mood scores (usually a number, one column for each day of the week)                          |
| Overall, how would you describe your recent mood?           | General mood in the last week (e.g., Good, Neutral, Low, Very Good)                                           |
| How many hours do you sleep on average per night?           | Self-reported average sleep per night                                                                         |
| Do you feel your social media/gaming habits affect your mood?| Participant's perception if their habits affect mood (Yes/No/Sometimes/Maybe)                                 |
| If yes, describe how (optional)                             | Open text entry to describe specific effect (if any)                                                          |
